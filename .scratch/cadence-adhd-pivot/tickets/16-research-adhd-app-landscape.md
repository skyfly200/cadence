## Question

Which popular todo/planner/coach apps are explicitly ADHD- or neurodivergent-oriented or widely loved by ADHD users (candidates to consider: Tiimo, Goblin.tools, Structured, Motion, Akiflow, Amazing Marvin, Inflow, Finch, Focusmate, Llama Life, Routine, Todoist, TickTick, Notion setups, Fabulous excluded as already covered), and for each: core mechanism, what ADHD users say works, what fails or gets abandoned and why (novelty decay, overwhelm, guilt), pricing/business model, and any AI or voice features? Produce a comparison table plus the recurring patterns of success and failure, and the gaps none of them fill relative to Cadence's vision (life graph, ambient nudges, receding when quiet, MCP capture).

## Resolution

Findings: branch `research/adhd-app-landscape`, file `research/adhd-app-landscape.md` (13-app comparison table plus newer entrants: Numo, Lifestack, Saner.AI, Focus One, Win Todo, DoThisNow, Lunatask; the latter thinly sourced).

- **What works:** near-zero capture cost, visible time, removing the "what next" decision, non-punishing tone, body doubling, on-demand task breakdown.
- **What fails:** novelty decay (asserted widely, no measured ADHD retention data found), setup overwhelm, plans without execution, over-rigid AI scheduling, paywalls and billing friction, feature bloat.
- **Closest competitors on capture:** Todoist Ramble (in-app voice capture, Jan 2026) and Akiflow's MCP connector (2026-06-30). Only Finch is non-punishing about lapses, and it is a wellness app, not a task system.
- **Gaps none fill:** life-graph dependencies, ambient spoken nudges, receding to a notepad when the user goes quiet.

Caveats: Reddit/forums unreachable, so "what users say" is second-hand from store reviews and review sites, some vendor marketing (tagged in the file). Several prices unverified (Todoist, TickTick, Llama Life, Routine, Tiimo, Structured).
