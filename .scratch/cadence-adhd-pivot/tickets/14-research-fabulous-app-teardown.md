## Question

How does the Fabulous app work (coaching tone and voice, journey/habit structure, onboarding, science claims, gamification and reward mechanics) and how did it change between the original version and later versions? Author's view: the original tone was excellent; later versions added too much pageantry and a cluttered, distracting UI/UX. What specifically worked, what was added that hurt, and what is transferable to an ADHD/AuDHD companion without the clutter?

## Resolution

Findings: branch `research/fabulous-app-teardown`, file `research/fabulous-app-teardown.md`.

- **What to keep (the voice you liked):** short, warm, second-person "letters" in a storybook frame; one tiny ask at a time with a reason ("put your shoes on and start your power song"); one habit at a time, starting with drinking water; unlocks gated on completion.
- **What went wrong:** recent reviews describe pop-ups, upsells, sister-app promotion, animation waits, and an ADHD user calls the interface "noisy and labyrinthine". Streaks with flames and Streak Freeze are brittle (no undo, no non-daily habits, unlogged days count as failed). Notifications can't be silenced selectively, and guilt-style pings and dismiss-only motivational images annoy users. When each addition arrived is inference; no release notes or redesign announcement was found.
- **Science claims:** the Duke/Ariely incubation origin is supported; the in-app "Duke study" and "96% changed their lives" are unsupported or self-reported; no independent trial found. The report also states a 2026-09-02 retraction of an Ariely 2002 paper, which I have not independently verified: treat as unconfirmed, but do not use Duke/Ariely prestige as a trust signal in Cadence copy either way.
- **Transfer:** borrow the tiny-ask-with-a-reason voice, one thing on screen at a time, forgiveness by default. Avoid per-completion pageantry, cross-promotion, upfront data harvesting, unsourced statistics, and ADHD marketing without ADHD-tested design.

Caveats: the agent did not use the app; Wayback and several Medium posts were blocked; no verbatim early in-app copy was retrievable, so early tone rests on 2016-2019 reviews and company posts. Third-party quotes need re-checking. Author's own memory of the original tone is the best remaining source: use it in the Coach role and persona grilling.
