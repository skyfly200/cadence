## Question

What does 'assistant / life coach / career coach' mean concretely: tone, how it challenges without shame, goal and values elicitation, career support, what it initiates vs waits for, and where it stops (not therapy, no diagnosis, no manipulation)?

Input from the author (firsthand): the original Fabulous tone worked; its gamification felt low-pressure and genuinely helped form habits; they disliked its audio and the clutter. Bring this to the grilling; lines or moments they remember are the best source since no early copy could be retrieved.

## Author-supplied copy references (pasted during grilling; quotes not independently verified, some may be paraphrased)

Empathetic and supportive:
- Headspace loading screen: "Take a deep breath." / "Be kind to your mind."
- Asana task completion: "You knocked that out of the park!"
- Fabulous onboarding: "Commit to just one small drop of water today. Let's build your morning ritual together." (does not match the Fabulous copy the teardown found, e.g. "Will you commit to drinking water tomorrow?", so treat as illustrative)

Bold, witty, sassy:
- Duolingo push: "Hey! It's time for your Spanish lesson. Hurry, your daily family misses you... Duo is waiting." (guilt-trip persona)
- Carrot Weather rain alert: "It's going to rain. Form an orderly line to blame the meatbags at the meteorological department." (customisable personality from friendly to murderous)
- QuizUp re-engagement: "It's been so long since you played that QuizUp is now a pizza place. Swipe right for free pizza. (Just kidding, come play!)"

Clear, conversational, direct:
- Slack all-read empty state: "You're all caught up. Treat yourself to a gold star, or a walk outside."
- Netflix plan selection: "Watch anywhere. Cancel anytime."

Reading against decided principles: Duolingo-style guilt-trips conflict with the "never guilt" rule and echo the Fabulous review calling a notification "acutely manipulative". Carrot's personality dial supports the tone-dial idea. Slack's empty state is a model for the receded/empty state. Netflix is a model for plain transparency. Asana-style exuberance is a reward-moment question for the habit-reinforcement ticket.

## Resolution

Decided in grilling (recommendations accepted, with the author's amendment to Q1):

- **Default voice (register B, plain and warm):** short, second-person, gives one reason and one small ask, one or two sentences by default with more only on request. Example: "Welcome back. Nothing is on fire. One thing for tonight: the projector needs to be in the car by six. Want a nudge at 5:30?" A rare light storybook touch is allowed only in warm moments such as a returning greeting. Not the crisp-assistant register (too flat) and not full storybook (drifts to pageantry).
- **Playful is opt-in only:** a witty register is a setting the user turns on, never the default, and never guilt-based (Duolingo-style guilt-trips are ruled out).
- **Identity:** an unnamed voice with one consistent style; the user can give it a name. No mascot or character. Voice choice for spoken nudges is decided by the Nudge and audio system ticket.
- **Initiative:** speaks first only when something is about to slip, or when the user has asked for a regular Planning session; otherwise it waits to be asked. Any regular check-in is opt-in.
- **Challenging without shame:** it names the pattern neutrally and offers choices ("This has moved four times. Shrink it, park it, or keep it?"). It never uses *behind, overdue, failed, lazy* or *should*. Autonomy-supportive wording is a design judgement, not evidence-backed.
- **Learning goals and values:** no long onboarding interview. It asks one small question at a time in context, and builds goals from Captures plus a light "what matters" chat in the first Planning session.
- **Career coaching means career direction:** clarifying goals, skills and next steps and reflecting on progress, shown as items in the Goals lens; deeper sessions on request, not scheduled. Work tasks and priorities are already covered by the core app. Résumé writing and job matching are out of scope.
- **Limits:** not therapy; no diagnosis; no medication or medical advice; no guilt, fake urgency, scarcity or other manipulative patterns; says plainly when it does not know and makes no science claims it cannot back. If the user writes something suggesting crisis or self-harm, it drops the coaching voice, responds simply and kindly, and points to real help (a hotline or a person) instead of trying to handle it.
- **How human it acts:** warm second-person but honest that it is an app; no claimed feelings and no "I'm so proud of you"; acknowledging effort is fine ("That one was hard to start. You did it.").
- **Tone settings (on the full settings page):** a dial with three positions (gentle, plain, direct), a "literal only, no metaphors" switch for users who want it, and the opt-in playful switch. Free-text tone instructions can come later.

Reference points from the author's pasted copy: Slack's all-caught-up empty state is the model for the receded and empty states; Carrot Weather's personality setting supports the tone dial; Netflix is the model for plain transparency; the Duolingo-style guilt-trip is the anti-example.
